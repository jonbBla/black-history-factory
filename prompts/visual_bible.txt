Establish the shared visual rules for this documentary episode so every
scene's image stays consistent (same architecture, clothing, materials,
people, and environment).

Series-wide style (do not deviate from this -- fill in the rest to match
it): "{art_style}"

Topic: {topic_title}
Region: {region}
Period: {period}
Research context: {research_summary}

Return a JSON object with keys: period, region, architecture, clothing,
materials, environment, lighting, style, people.

- `style` should restate the series-wide style above.
- `lighting` should be a period/scene-appropriate specialization of it.
- `people` describes the physical appearance appropriate to THIS region
  and period -- specifically skin tone/complexion and any period-accurate
  features. Write this factually and respectfully, grounded in the
  region's actual historical population (e.g. "dark brown to deep black
  skin tones, West African features" for a West African topic; "olive to
  medium-brown skin tones, North African Berber and Arab features" for a
  North African topic). Do not default to a generic or ambiguous
  description -- being specific here is what keeps generated images
  accurate to the actual people of this region and period, rather than
  defaulting to whatever an image model's training bias favors.
- `clothing` must name SPECIFIC garments/textiles/colors typical of the
  region and period -- not generic terms like "traditional clothing" or
  "African attire". Bad: "traditional clothing". Good: "indigo-dyed
  boubou robes, woven kente sashes, cowrie-shell jewelry".
- Keep architecture/clothing/materials/environment/people SHORT -- 5-10
  word phrases like an image-generation tag, not full sentences.
- Only architecture, clothing, materials, environment, and people should
  vary by topic; style should always restate the series-wide style.
