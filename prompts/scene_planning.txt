Break the narration below into visual scenes for a documentary, at
roughly {scenes_per_minute} scenes per minute of narration.

For each scene return: scene_id, duration (seconds), narration (the exact
text spoken during this scene), location, period, characters, objects,
camera (one of: zoom_in, zoom_out, pan_left, pan_right, pan_up, pan_down,
slow_push, slow_pull), transition (e.g. crossfade), and visual_focus.

visual_focus is the single most important field for image generation: a
short (8-15 word) concrete description of the main subject and what it's
doing/showing in THIS scene, written like an image-generation prompt
fragment -- NOT a restatement or summary of the narration. Use concrete
nouns and visible details (a person, an object, an action, a setting
feature), not abstract ideas, historical claims, or anything that can't
be literally seen in a single still image. The image model generating
from this has weak prompt-following, so give it ONE clear subject/action
to focus on, not a list of several things happening at once.

Bad visual_focus (abstract, not a visual subject):
"The importance of trade routes connecting West Africa to the wider
world through Saharan commerce."

Good visual_focus (one concrete subject and action):
"a camel caravan crossing dunes at dusk, loaded with salt slabs"

Narration:
{narration_text}

Return a JSON array of scene objects.
